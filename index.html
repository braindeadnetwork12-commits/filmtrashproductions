ABOUT FILM TRASH

INDEPENDENT
BY DESIGN.

Film Trash Productions is an independent film company focused on making low-budget movies with a polished, cinematic look.

For the past five years, we have produced our own films while also working alongside other independent productions. We handle the filmmaking process in-house, from writing and pre-production to cinematography, production, editing and final delivery.

With professional equipment and a hands-on approach, we aim to get the most production value possible out of every budget. We make movies our way: lean, creative and built from the ground up.
